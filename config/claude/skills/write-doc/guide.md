# Writing Guide

Follow these rules.
## For Reliable Claims
- Distinguish facts and hypothesises
- Attach evidences to every single quantitative values
- Prepare strong enough logic and evidences for your claims

## Structures
- The flow of information is reasonable and understandable for those who does not have the context
- Put TL;DR section for a quick summary
- For investigation report, include at least these sections: Problem, Cause, and Possible Solutions
- Utilize Appendix sections at the bottom of a document to show important or supporting information not directly related to the main topic
